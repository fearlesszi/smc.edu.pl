Admissions & Credentials

Join a self-directed learning community built around curiosity, action and independent judgment.

Institutional identity clarification
This website identifies as Sovereign Modern College. It is distinct from Malta-based Signum Magnum College. MFHEA institution licence 2019-014 belongs to Signum Magnum College and must not be represented as a licence held by this website merely because both use the initials “SMC”. State the actual institution accurately when applying for education offers. MFHEA quality audit report · WHED institutional record

Who we welcome

SMC does not use a single entrance threshold based on national examination scores, prior formal qualifications or age. Applicants describe their interests, a real-world problem they want to address, and the work they are prepared to undertake. Applications are reviewed by SMC; submission does not guarantee admission.

Auditor · Free

Follow public learning: subscribe to college publications, attend open seminars when scheduled, and explore open research resources.

Matriculated Student

Take available courses, join practical workshops and maintain a learning record. Fees, credit rules and any academic email service are subject to published policy and actual provisioning.

SMC Founding Fellow

Proposed honours pathway: complete 60 credits and deliver at least one 400-level capstone project. The title is awarded only after SMC review; it is not self-declared.

Tuition & grants

Per-course fees, an annual All-Access Pass and grant terms have not yet been published. No estimated prices are presented as official. A Founding Pioneer Grant may be established for substantial open-source work or research proposals; terms require formal announcement.

Education email and third-party offers
An institutional email address does not guarantee a third-party education discount. Each provider independently assesses institutional eligibility, current student status, region and permitted use. SMC does not guarantee approval by any provider.
