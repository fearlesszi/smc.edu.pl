The SMC Charter

Intellectual autonomy is the ability to frame questions, examine assumptions and reason independently. Rigour means testing ideas through research, prototypes, case analysis and documented work. Pluralism joins freedom of inquiry with respect for persons and responsibility for consequences. Knowledge should be open where rights and safety permit, and claims should rest on inspectable sources. Learning is a lifelong, interdisciplinary practice.
