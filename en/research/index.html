A developing programme for working papers, essays, methodological notes and projects. Only materials formally released by SMC will appear as publications.

Editorial principles / Zasady redakcyjne

  • Transparent authorship, affiliation and contribution statements.
  • Distinction between peer-reviewed research, working papers, essays and project reports.
  • References, data provenance and methods made inspectable wherever possible.
  • Ethical review and privacy safeguards appropriate to each project.
  • Corrections and version history recorded for substantive revisions.
