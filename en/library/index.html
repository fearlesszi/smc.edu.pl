The SMC Library

A curated reading room connecting foundational texts, research literacy, primary sources and interdisciplinary pathways. A catalogue will follow review of holdings and rights.

Collection principles

  • Prefer primary sources, scholarly editions and transparent research repositories.
  • Provide bibliographic metadata and stable identifiers where available.
  • Respect copyright, licence terms, privacy and the rights of authors and publishers.
  • Distinguish recommended reading from material formally adopted in a course.
The catalogue is being prepared. Titles, access links and reading lists will be added only after bibliographic and rights review.
